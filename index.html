You have three ammo cells. They reload automatically. Stop attacking and avoid damage for four seconds to regenerate health. Your super charges slowly and faster by dealing damage or healing. Gadgets activate once per cooldown after unlocking. No gadgets or passives are active at level 1. Gadgets unlock at levels 4, 7 and 9; passives at levels 6, 8 and 10. Equip one of each after it unlocks.

Choose a class to see its five fighters. Each has different stats, attack patterns and a unique super. Rook has almost no attack range. Jolt has mid-range burst damage. Sprig’s shots heal wounded teammates or hurt enemies. Fuse lobs over walls. Wisp is fast and fragile. Marksmen have medium health and very long-range shots, but cannot shoot through walls. Bushes conceal enemies unless they are close or reveal themselves by attacking or taking damage.

Hot Zone is 3 vs 3 with respawns; a contested zone gives no progress. Duel and Knockout have no respawns during rounds. The first team to two round wins wins. A closing danger ring prevents endless hiding; a round timeout compares remaining health.

Progress is stored on this browser, not a server account. Online queues require 2 or 6 real players. Friends rooms can start with fewer humans and add bots. Disconnecting players are replaced by bots so the match continues.
