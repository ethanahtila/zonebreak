# ZONEBREAK fighter guide — 1.0.1

Base stats below are for level 1. Supers work at level 1. Gadget choices unlock at 4 / 7 / 9; passive choices at 6 / 8 / 10. Class members share three gadget and passive effect families, while their stats, basic attacks and supers create different playstyles.

## Tanks

| Fighter | HP / speed / range | Basic attack and playstyle | Super |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Rook | 7600 / 205 / 100 | **Pile Driver** — A walking fortress. Devastating at arm’s length, but needs cover to close the gap. | **Faultline** — Charge forward, slam nearby enemies, and gain a brief shield. |
| Bulwark | 8300 / 188 / 90 | **Hammer Sweep** — The slowest, toughest tank. A broad hammer sweep and a protective team shockwave. | **Stand Together** — Deal 1,250 damage in a wide shockwave and shield nearby allies for 3 seconds. |
| Bramble | 7200 / 215 / 110 | **Thorn Claw** — A control tank whose thorn strikes slow enemies. Vines deny a patch of ground. | **Entangle** — Grow a 5-second thorn field at your feet, slowing enemies and dealing 300 damage per second. |
| Kiln | 7000 / 210 / 105 | **Forge Fist** — A furnace in armor. Narrow, forceful punches give way to a short-range flame burst. | **Open Furnace** — Fire six piercing flames in a short cone for up to 2,400 damage. |
| Anchor | 7800 / 198 / 95 | **Deck Sweep** — A wide-swinging brawler. A short grappling line pulls an opponent into striking distance. | **Reel In** — Launch a wall-blocked hook up to 290 units, dealing 1,500 damage and pulling the target toward you. |

Gadget effects: **Level 4: Iron Skin** — Take 40% less damage for 3 seconds. (17s cooldown); **Level 7: Second Wind** — Instantly restore 22% of maximum health. (22s cooldown); **Level 9: Fortress March** — Move 35% faster for 2.5 seconds. (20s cooldown).

Passive effects: **Level 6: Last Stand** — Take 15% less damage while below 35% health.; **Level 8: Heavy Hands** — Basic hits briefly slow the target.; **Level 10: Momentum** — Move 8% faster while above 70% health..
